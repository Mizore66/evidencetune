Protocol and claim boundaries

Recorded evidence
All responses are saved raw outputs. Condition controls replay local text; they do not invoke live inference. The complete payload is embedded byte-for-byte in this file.
Selection chronology
Selection used dev only; frozen results were confirmation-only. No new two-arm merged-confirmation generation occurred before selection.json was locked. Earlier v7/v8 frozen runs existed but were not used for merged-model selection. These are not the project’s first frozen runs.
Backend and scorer
Both current arms use local Apple MPS bf16 Transformers and custom EvidenceTune contract-v2 scorers, not AuditKIT. Historical Kaggle fp16, RTX and vLLM results are not mixed into these tables.
Validation boundary
Labels are synthetic construction proxies, not independent human sufficiency annotations. Issue #12 labels remain independent_ai_review until qualified humans approve them.
Unmeasured and unresolved
No base→merged correction improvement is claimed because raw base Tiny Aya was not evaluated on these suites. Contradiction and Arabic weaknesses remain; this page makes no broad Arabic or natural-domain performance claim.
Rejected directions
DPO and natural SciFact training were rejected. CircuitKIT’s smoke result applies to original v6 only.
